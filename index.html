Which market gives the best first season?

Every fee-paying permanent summer signing from 2016-17 onward, scored on their first Fantasy Premier League season at the buying club. This compares selling countries across all ten seasons and all clubs, so it ignores the filters above.

The same measure, season by season

Only England signs enough players each year for a single season to mean much (23–51 a year). Everyone else averages two to eight, so a year can hinge on one player. Seasons with fewer than four signings are drawn as hollow dots on a dashed line — read those as anecdotes, not trend.
